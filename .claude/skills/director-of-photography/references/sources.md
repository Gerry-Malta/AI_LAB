# Sources

Books (the canon this skill condenses)
- Joseph V. Mascelli, *The Five C's of Cinematography: Motion Picture Filming Techniques* (1965; Silman-James).
  https://www.silmanjamespress.com/catalogs/the-five-cs-of-cinematography — summary:
  https://www.robertcmorton.com/the-5-cs-of-cinematography/
- Daniel Arijon, *Grammar of the Film Language* (Silman-James): the triangle principle, external / internal reverse
  and parallel camera positions. https://www.silmanjamespress.com/shop/filmmaking-directing/grammar-of-the-film-language/ ·
  a practitioner's account of the triangle set-up: https://www.shootfirst.art/the-triangle-system
- Bruce Block, *The Visual Story* (contrast and affinity; the seven basic visual components; graphing visual
  intensity against story intensity). https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/the-visual-story/9780240807799/009_9780080551692_chapter2.html ·
  course notes: https://cloud.wikis.utexas.edu/wiki/spaces/rtf318/pages/80021989/Graphing+Visual+Structure
- David Bordwell, "Intensified Continuity", *Film Quarterly* 55:3 (2002) — faster cutting, closer framings, bipolar
  lens lengths, the free-ranging camera. https://www.davidbordwell.net/blog/2007/05/27/intensified-continuity-revisited/
